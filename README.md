

# Blinkit Sales & Marketing Dashboard

## 📊 Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive view of Blinkit's performance across sales, inventory, marketing, and customer feedback. It is designed to help stakeholders monitor business growth and identify opportunities for improvement.

## 🧩 Features

- **Sales Overview:** Monthly comparison of total orders amount (TOA) for current and previous year, with growth trend line.
- **Inventory Monitoring:** Visualization of stock received vs. damaged stock.
- **Marketing Analytics:** Key metrics like impressions, clicks, conversions, revenue, and profit.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Categorizes customers into Regular, Premium, New, and Inactive groups.
- **Customer Feedback:** Highlights most dissatisfied customers with emoji indicators for quick insight.
- **Category Filters:** Dynamic slicer to analyze specific product categories.
- **Growth KPI:** YOY growth % shown as a KPI card.
- **Responsive Design:** Slicers and filters allow interactive exploration.

## 🛠️ Built With

- Microsoft Power BI
- Data Modeling (Star Schema)
- DAX Measures
- Interactive Visuals
